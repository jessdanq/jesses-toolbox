03 / INSTAGRAM TOOLS

audience.

Discover who doesn't follow you back on Instagram. Completely private, calculated directly on your device with zero account logins, no browser cookies, and zero risk of account flags.

How to get your Instagram files (takes ~1 minute) 💡
  1. In Instagram, open Settings & Privacy → Accounts Center → Your information and permissions.
  2. Tap Download your information → Download or transfer information.
  3. Choose Some of your information and check only Followers and following.
  4. Set Format to JSON and Date range to All time, then tap Create files.
  5. Download and unzip the file, then drop followers_1.json and following.json below.
